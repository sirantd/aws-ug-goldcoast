title: New directions guide — how to find us at Cohort
date: 2026-10-20
---

Never been to our meetups before? We've added a step-by-step directions map to the
homepage so you know exactly where to go. Look for the colourful COHORT building on
Nexus Way, walk in and turn left — the Board-room is right there. The
[Griffith University G:link stop](https://translink.com.au/plan-your-journey/network-information/light-rail)
is a short walk south if you're coming by tram.
